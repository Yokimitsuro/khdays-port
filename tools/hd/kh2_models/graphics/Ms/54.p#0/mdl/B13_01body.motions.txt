# The game's animations played as KH2 motions: `<game animation> <mset slot>`, one a line.
# The slot is the entry's number in the mset (0 first). Anything not listed keeps the game's animation.
# KHDAYS_TRACE_MODELS=1 logs the names of the game's animations as they play.
# This model is the coat Riku (N_EX950) as the boss Riku's body (Ms/54.p's B13_01body; Mission Mode's A_B13_01body
# takes these settings), on riku_h's bones (his skeleton is the player's node for node). His animations are
# B13_01body_001-030: ov260 plays pose p as clip p + 2 (Ov260_SetPose), B13_01body_<p + 1>. Only KH2's idle, run and
# damage carry over from the playable Riku's mset (P_EH000); everything else is the game's own.
mset P_EH000.mset
# off: the game places him and KH2's root motion is taken out of the bones (as KH2 does)
root_motion off
# The KH2 keyblade (W_EX010_20, drawn by the game's sword object B13_02w, `held kh2_2 body`) on KH2's bone past the right
# hand (262), as riku_h's. The game never reads sa_h_R (Ov260_Construct looks up Bip01 and Bip01_L_Hand only): it draws
# the sword object at his base, its own animation keeping its grip (wA) 7-11 cm from his right hand, turned at most 6
# degrees off one grip in all 30 animations, so the KH2 hand holds it throughout
weapon sa_h_R 262 rotate 0 -90 90
# What he holds plays W_EH000.mset's slots as his own play P_EH000's (on W_EH000's bones; bone 2 the blade)
weapon_motions W_EH000.mset W_EH000.mdlx 2
# His energy sword once he powers up (B13E011, Ms/54.p#55: Ov260_Construct's +0x38c part, shown in his stance), drawn
# at his base like the sword object, its node EF_B13E01 0.4-1.5 cm and under 2 degrees off the sword's grip (wA) in
# all 30 animations, its blade along +z (wA's tip tag00 at z 0.87): put on the KH2 keyblade's grip bone (W_EH000's
# bone 2 as drawn), its blade along that bone's -x (the keychain, bones 3-11, hangs along +x): x to y, y to -z, z to -x
weapon_model Ms/54.p EF_B13E01 weapon rotate -90 0 90

# The game's own animations have no fingers: these turn as slot 2 (the calm idle) turns them at its first frame;
# the back hair turned out of the hood, as riku_h's
ds_pose 2 135-156 263-284
ds_pose rotate 0 0 30 172

# ov260 cuts from one animation into the next (Ov260_BindClip drops the clip and binds the new one at frame 0), so
# every change is faded here from the pose last shown over 8 of the game's frames. Only what is drawn changes: the
# game's animations, frames and timing are its own
cut_blend 8
# but into his damage (user): cut into it, into its aerial damage and into its landing; faded out of all of them
cut_blend_except B13_01body_005 B13_01body_006

# His idle (001, pose 0): KH2's battle idle
B13_01body_001 0 start_loop blend 8
idle B13_01body_001
# His run (003, pose 2) and the 6-frame clips into it and out of it (002 from AiEnterTakeOff, 004 from WalkTick):
# the run start plays KH2's run, the stop KH2's idle, each carried on by the animation after it (`start_loop`)
B13_01body_002 8 blend 8
B13_01body_003 8 start_loop blend 8
B13_01body_004 0 blend 8

# Damage (Ov260_SwipeEntry: pose 4 hit from the front, 005; pose 5 from behind, 006), KH2's front and back. As the
# player's `finish 24`, KH2's readying (24) and battle idle (0) follow while the game still plays it (`after`), cut in
# as KH2's damage ends on its calm idle; his AI moving on cuts it off, faded (`cut_blend`)
B13_01body_005 42 after 24 0
B13_01body_006 50 after 24 0
# Damage in the air, by the game's floor contact (`air_floor`: the boss runs none of the player's actor states):
# KH2's aerial damage loops (66 front, 74 back) while he is off the floor; touching down while the game still plays
# the damage, KH2's landing (70, 78) plays, then readies and idles as on the ground, until the game moves on
air_floor on

air B13_01body_006 74 land 78 after 24 0
