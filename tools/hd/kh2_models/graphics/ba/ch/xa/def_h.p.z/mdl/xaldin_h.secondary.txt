# Secondary motion of KH2's Xaldin (B_EX130) drawn as Days' xaldin_h and xaldin: axel_h's coat on the same bones, his

# --- The lower coat: eight strips from the waist, one connected surface (axel_h's, the same bones) ---
# Each strip runs from just under the belt to the hem. 85 is shared: the front
# forks under it into two panels (88-90 and 92-94) with the slit between them.
group coat surface preset kh2_coat
  attach 2
  strip 26 29 30 31 32
  strip 34 37 38 39 40
  strip 42 45 46 47 48
  strip 50 53 54 55 56
  strip 58 61 62 63 64
  strip 66 69 70 71 72
  strip 74 77 78 79 80
  strip 82 85 88 89 90
  strip 85 92 93 94
  # the thighs carry the coat's shape: each control's target rides them by how near it
  # hangs to them at rest (up to 70%, none 38 cm away: axel_h's 34, his coat as much wider), so a wide stance spreads
  # the coat in its target and contact only catches what is left; a thigh coming up into it (a crouch, a high step)
  # carries the panels near it up to 90%. Not the shins: below the knee the coat falls straight down off it
  follow_bone 5 radius 38 weight 0.9 always 0.7
  follow_bone 14 radius 38 weight 0.9 always 0.7
  topology mesh           # neighbours from the triangles skinned to them
  disconnect 88 92        # the front slit stays open
  # the back strips (26-32, 50-56, 74-80) ride the thighs less, the less the lower: the back hangs
  bone 29 follow_bone_scale 0.6
  bone 30 follow_bone_scale 0.4
  bone 31 follow_bone_scale 0.3
  bone 32 follow_bone_scale 0.2
  bone 53 follow_bone_scale 0.6
  bone 54 follow_bone_scale 0.4
  bone 55 follow_bone_scale 0.3
  bone 56 follow_bone_scale 0.2
  bone 77 follow_bone_scale 0.6
  bone 78 follow_bone_scale 0.4
  bone 79 follow_bone_scale 0.3
  bone 80 follow_bone_scale 0.2
  mode simulated
end

# --- Hood: one bone (101) hanging behind the neck; it swings and lies on the back as one piece. Against the capsules
# on his back, not the torso's: the chest's capsule, fitted to his 54 cm wide chest, reaches back past the hood's root
# (z -25), so the hood started inside it and was pushed out every tick (it jittered) ---
group hood chain preset kh2_hood
  collide hair_torso head
  attach 99
  chain 101
  settle on steps 240
  mode simulated
end

# --- The back hair piece (171-177) is not simulated: it stays on his head as the animation (or its rest) holds it, so
# its three braids hang from where it is. Simulated with the braids, it swung and pulled them into his head. The braids
# (178-179, 181-182, 184-185) are drawstrings (kh2_string), from their `ds_pose` turn (motions.txt), against his head,
# back (the hood lying on it), shoulders, neck and the hood itself ---
group back_braids chain preset kh2_string
  collide head hair_torso hair_neck hair_hood
  thickness 1.5
  # a third of the drawstrings' inertia and more damping: his hovering idle bobs the head, and at the drawstrings' own
  # values that threw the braids into his back every few frames (23 cm p99 jitter in the replay's idle; now 6)
  inertia 0.3
  damping 4
  # no swing limit: the drawstrings' 75 degrees from the pose's way, in his forward-leaning run (b_xa_A001), threw a
  # back braid to the cone's far side once a stride (115 cm twitch in the replay; now 4)
  swing_limit 180
  attach 176
  chain 178 179
  chain 181 182
  chain 184 185
  settle on steps 360
  mode simulated
end

# --- Animation controls ---
# B_EX130's own motion carries its coat and drawstrings as KH2 animated them: there they guide the cloth, and the thighs
# carry none of it. The game's own animations keep the cloth on the hips and thighs.
control own_motions
  animation *
  slot 0-255
  groups coat string_l string_r
  mode guided
  follow_frequency 6 hem 3
  follow_damping 0.8
  follow_bone_scale 0
  blend 0.15 0.15
end

# His KH2 motion (slot 0, the hover he flies in) keys his braids blowing in the wind: there they follow it, as hair does
# (kh2_hair's pull), rather than hanging under gravity. The game's own animations leave them simulated.
control own_hair
  animation *
  slot 0-255
  groups back_braids braids
  mode guided
  follow_frequency 6
  follow_damping 0.7
  blend 0.15 0.15
end
