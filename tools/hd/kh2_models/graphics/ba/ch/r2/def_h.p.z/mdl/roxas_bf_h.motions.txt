# The game's animations played as KH2 motions: `<game animation> <mset slot>`, one a line.
# The slot is the entry's number in the mset (0 first). Anything not listed keeps the game's animation.
# KHDAYS_TRACE_MODELS=1 logs the names of the game's animations as they play.
# This model is the hooded coat Roxas (B_EX390) as Days' dual wield Roxas (ba/ch/r2, roxas_bf_h). It plays
# KH2's own motions for it (B_EX390.mset, slots 0-256, on its own bones) and the street-clothes Roxas's
# (P_EX110.mset from slot 1000, GAMEOVER.mset's AK10 from 2000), retargeted onto it as for roxas_ho_h.
mset B_EX390.mset own
motions P_EX110.mset at 1000
# GAMEOVER.mset holds a set per character; AK10's slot 14 (entry 0140) is Roxas's game over, here 2014
motions GAMEOVER.mset AK10 at 2000
# off: the game places Roxas and KH2's root motion is taken out of the bones (as KH2 does);
# on: the body also travels as the KH2 motion moves it.
root_motion off
# What the game holds at its weapon joints (ro_h_L, ro_h_R) on B_EX390's keyblade bones (95 left, 106
# right), turned from their axes into the frame the game's keyblades are made in (x, then y, then z,
# degrees): the blade along the bone's -x as B_EX390's own blades lie, its flat facing the bone's -z
# (the turn of roxas_ho_h's `weapon ro_w_tg_R 222 rotate 0 -90 90`, onto 106's axes). The game's own
# animations hold them as Days does (bones.txt places 95 and 106 on its weapon joints).
weapon ro_h_L 95 rotate 180 90 0
weapon ro_h_R 106 rotate 180 90 0
# In P_EX110's motions the keyblade bones ride its weapon bones (149 left, 222 right) as B_EX390's slot 0
# holds them (B_EX390 keeps 95 on 149 and 106 on 222 in every motion: 5 cm out, the same turn)
carry 95 149 at 0
carry 106 222 at 0
