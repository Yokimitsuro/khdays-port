# This model is Xigbar (B_EX140) as Days' xigbar_h and xigbar, playing KH2's own motions for him (`own`: on his own
# bones). Its slots: 0 idle (00A), 1 and 2 the walk (01A both: no run of its own), 10 the damage from the front (20A),
# 11 from behind (22A); no jump, fall or landing (3 and 6-9 are empty, 4, 5, 20 and 21 hold 00A again).

# cm up at rest's 14.8, his walk forward along +z as any other's. Turned half about his forward axis (z) at 117.5 cm,
# (x, y, z) to (-x, 235 - y, z): on his feet, the soles on the floor. The root motion and the feet on the floor
# (IKAdjust) turn with them.
