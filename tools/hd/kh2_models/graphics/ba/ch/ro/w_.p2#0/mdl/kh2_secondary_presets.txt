# Reusable secondary-motion presets for KH2 characters (docs/SECONDARY_MOTION.md).
# Style settings only: no bone numbers. A character's file includes this one
# (`include kh2_secondary_presets.txt`) and names the presets in its groups.
# Tuning seeds measured against B_EX390's baked coat and refined in the replay
# tool, not KH2's own parameters (KH2 bakes its cloth into the motions).
secondary_motion 1
units cm

# A long coat: barely stretches, bends with resistance so neighbouring strips
# fold together, moves broadly and settles in about a second. The plain values
# hold at the waist; `hem` values are reached at the free edge.
preset kh2_coat
  mass 0.06
  stretch_compliance 0.0005
  # around the rows: a skirt flares like a dress spinning out, its circumference growing toward the hem
  # (KH2's baked coats do this); stiff and hardly stretching at the belt, free at the hem, so a panel
  # over a raised knee is not dragged back by its neighbours
  row_compliance 0.00002 hem 0.05 curve 1.5
  # along a strip: stretches where a leg comes up into it (KH2's own coat stretches about 2x in the
  # battle idle); held at the rest length it fights the leg, and the coat twitches
  max_strain 0.6
  row_max_strain 0.04 hem 4 curve 1.5
  shear_compliance 0.0002
  bend_compliance 0.0015
  damping 2.5
  # the pull toward the shape on the hips, weak toward the hem: that shape tips with the pelvis (in a
  # crouch it lies behind the knees, under the floor), so the lower coat falls off the knee instead
  follow_frequency 4.0 hem 0.3 curve 1.5
  follow_damping 0.55
  gravity_scale 1
  tether_slack 0.03
  swing_limit 55 hem 70
  twist_limit 0                      # no roll about the bone's length: swings only
  inertia 1.0 hem 0.9
  guided_inertia 0.4
  max_speed 12
  # the drawn shape eased toward the solved one (in the hips' space, so the body is never delayed):
  # contact against a leg sweeping through the coat jumps step to step, this hides it. A drawn face
  # that would cut into a leg it trails is moved out (draw_guard; 0 turns that off)
  smoothing 0.08
  draw_guard 1
  thickness 1.5
  friction 0.3
  # a face resting on a knee holds to it instead of sliding round it (the coat climbed the knee in
  # the battle idle and dropped back off)
  face_friction 0.9
  wind_drag 1.5
  collide body legs         # the waist, hips and legs only
  self_collision garment
end

# Short sculpted hair: close on the animation's own (keyed) hair motion, a little
# follow-through. Guided: the animation's own hair motion is the target.
preset kh2_hair
  # P_EX110's motions key the hair's sway (2-3 degrees in the calm idle at 0.3 Hz, 3-19 in the run at
  # 2.8-3.8 Hz, 15-45 rms in attacks), so it follows that closely: 6 Hz at 0.7 of critical damping passes
  # it with little lag and no resonance, a fifth of the body's acceleration adds a little follow-through.
  # Slower springs lagged the keyed sway and wobbled on their own (jelly); 7 Hz with a 9 degree limit
  # shook against the limit (rubbery)
  mode guided
  mass 0.01
  stretch_compliance 0
  damping 3
  follow_frequency 6
  follow_damping 0.7
  gravity_scale 0.3
  swing_limit 15
  twist_limit 0
  inertia 0.6
  guided_inertia 0.2
  max_speed 4
  thickness 0.5
  friction 0
  wind_drag 0.4
  # its bones only turn: a tuft lagging in a fast move no longer pulls its root off the head
  rotate_only 1
  collide head
end

# The flat hair cards on the back of the head (and the tuft they hang on):

  mode guided

  rotate_only 1

end
