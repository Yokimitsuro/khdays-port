# Two-bone limbs, knees and elbows bending the way Days' do. `exact` (the legs): the feet go where the game's are. The
# offset keeps KH2's sole on the game's: his ankle is 14.9 over its sole, the game's 13.0 (the 1.6 is roxas_bf_h's).
ik kh2_5 kh2_6 kh2_9 exact 0 1.9 1.6
ik kh2_14 kh2_15 kh2_18 exact 0 1.9 1.6

# In the game's own animations the body lowered until each leg reaches as the game's does for its length, so
# the knees bend as the game's: the legs alone straightened where the game's bent (his left knee 144 degrees in the game's Ca006 (the game's 135))
lower_hips 1
ik kh2_112 kh2_113 kh2_119
ik kh2_199 kh2_200 kh2_206

# The pelvis's vertices thinner front to back (its local y points backward), so the hips stay under the coat, as on
# roxas_bf_h; the bone itself, the legs and the cloth's colliders are unchanged.
mesh_scale kh2_2 1 0.9 1
# The thighs' vertices 10% thinner front to back too (their y points backward), as riku_h's, so they stay under
# the coat; the thigh colliders (secondary.txt) are sized on them.
mesh_scale kh2_5 1 0.9 1
mesh_scale kh2_14 1 0.9 1

# The pelvis under the coat not drawn, as riku_h's: what kh2_2 carries from below where the coat itself is skinned to
# it (B_EX110's coat on kh2_2 from 117 cm up) down to the hip joints (108), faded back in over 4 cm
hide kh2_2 from 115 to 108 fade 4

# `align`: the hands and feet turned onto the game's at rest by their shape.
align kh2_9 foot
align kh2_18 foot
align kh2_119 hand
align kh2_206 hand

# The weapon bones (147 left, 234 right) on the game's weapon joints, exactly where they are, so what the game holds
# there is where Days puts it. `ride`: moved by how far the KH2 hand is from the game's (his shoulders stand 11 cm
# higher, his arms are longer), so they stand from his hands as the game's from its, and a throw still leaves them.
# The hands stay as they are (a plain `place` would stretch each hand toward its joint, its only paired child).
kh2_147 ax_h_L
kh2_234 ax_h_R
place kh2_147 ride
place kh2_234 ride

# B_EX110's two weapons are part of its mesh, in his hands (bones 147-148 and 234-235). Their triangles are not drawn;
