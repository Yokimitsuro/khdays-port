# The port's compositions for the one-screen picture (docs/COMPOSITIONS.md: the
# lines), a layout of its own for each of the game's screens, each made from
# the game's pieces measured on its screens. The field's HUD on one screen, laid
# out for the PC (asked for on 2026-10-08, after the maintainer's sketch):
# the mission's panel at the top left with its chests and badges in small
# boxes of their own in its right end, the map in the top right corner with
# the enemy's health over it, the command deck at the bottom left, the party at the
# bottom right, and the INFORMATION banner at the top left, above everything,
# only for a few seconds after it changes. Every piece is the game's own,
# taken from where the DS draws it (the regions measured on the screens of
# missions 17 and 18, 2026-10-08).
#
# The pieces' black ground (the map's white, turned black) is seen through at
# 0.65 (`see-through`, the port's effect), their text, edges and icons not.
#
# Units are the port's (`units port`): a margin of M is M picture dots and a
# scale of S makes a DS dot S of them, both times the HUD's factor (1 at the
# default HUD size; ui/composition.c's table for the others); `fixed(N)` does
# not follow it. A piece of a box is placed by its margin: the box's margin
# plus its offset in the box times the scale.
